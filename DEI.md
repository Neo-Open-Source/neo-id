# Diversity, Equity & Inclusion

## Our Commitment

Neo ID is committed to creating an inclusive environment where everyone can contribute. We value diverse perspectives and believe the best products are built by teams that reflect the communities they serve.

## What We Expect

### Communication

- Use respectful, professional language in all interactions
- Avoid assumptions about someone's background, experience, or identity
- Provide constructive feedback focused on code and ideas, not people
- Welcome newcomers and help them get oriented

### Reviews

- Review code objectively — evaluate the work, not the author
- Ask clarifying questions before making assumptions
- Acknowledge different approaches and experience levels
- Be explicit about what is blocking vs. what is a suggestion

### Issues & Discussions

- Label issues with `good-first-issue` for newcomers
- Respond to questions within 48 hours
- Document decisions and rationale publicly
- Avoid gatekeeping — explain context, don't just say "it's obvious"

## What We Do Not Accept

- Harassment, slurs, or personal attacks
- Discriminatory language or imagery
- Unsolicited comments about someone's appearance, background, or identity
- Retaliation against anyone who reports an issue in good faith
- Dismissive responses to valid concerns

## Reporting

If you experience or witness a violation:

1. **Public**: Open an issue with the `conduct` label
2. **Private**: Email the maintainers directly

All reports are taken seriously and handled confidentially. We will investigate and respond within 72 hours.

## Enforcement

Violations may result in:

- Warning
- Temporary suspension from the project
- Permanent ban

The severity depends on the nature and impact of the violation. We prioritize restoration and education over punishment, but will act decisively when necessary.

## Accessibility

- All documentation should be readable by screen readers
- Use semantic HTML in components
- Provide alt text for images
- Ensure sufficient color contrast (WCAG AA minimum)
- Do not rely solely on color to convey information
